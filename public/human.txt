Hi. If you're reading this, you probably want to understand how a
CopperSmith SKU is put together without using the live builder. This is
that explanation.

The live tool is at https://sku.thecoppersmith.net — use it if you can,
it does all this work for you. But if you're old school or you're
trying to read a SKU on a packing slip and figure out what it means,
this is the formula.


================================================================
THE FORMULA
================================================================

A buildable SKU is the base SKU followed by a series of option codes,
joined with hyphens, in this order:

    Base SKU
      + Finish
      + Mount
      + Companion (only if the mount needs one)
      + Decorative
      + Electric or Gas options (last)

Every segment past Base SKU is optional. The default finish (Antique
Copper) doesn't add a suffix — it's just absent.


================================================================
THE PIECES
================================================================

1. PARENT SKU (e.g. AS-E)
   This is NOT in the final SKU. It's how products are grouped under
   catalog listings. If you see "AS-E" written anywhere, it means "the
   Adams Street Electric line." It's not a thing you can order.

2. BASE SKU (e.g. AS41E)
   This is where the actual orderable SKU begins. Three parts:
     - Family code (e.g. AS for Adams Street)
     - Size in inches (e.g. 41)
     - Ignition type (E for Electric, G for Gas)

   Every base SKU ships as a wall-mounted fixture with a standard
   backplate by default. The base SKU alone is a complete, orderable
   product.

3. FINISH (e.g. -BLK)
   Five options:
     (none)   Antique Copper — the default, no suffix added
     -BLK     Matte Black
     -GRAY    Graphite Gray
     -BRZ     Oil Rubbed Bronze
     -CLEAR   Clear Coat (white in the UI swatch)

   The AOB family (Biltmore Aurora Brass) is brass only — its SKUs
   never get a finish suffix because the "B" already encodes brass.

4. MOUNT (e.g. -WY, -TS3, -CY05, -PF1)
   You pick options from ONE mount category per SKU. The categories
   are mutually exclusive on a single SKU:

     Wall Mount:             Wall Yoke, Gooseneck Plain, Gooseneck Scroll,
                             Grand Harbor 12", Grand Harbor 17",
                             Estate Extension, the three Moustaches
                             (Classic / Dual Scroll / Fancy)

     Wall Mount Accessories: Top Scroll, Top Scroll Wide,
                             Bottom Scroll, Bottom Scroll Wide,
                             Reverse Bottom Scroll,
                             Farm House Hook, Bottom Farm House Hook,
                             Moneo Top Hook

     Ceiling & Hanging:      Classic Yoke, Pendant Yoke,
                             Contemporary Yoke, Stem Mount,
                             Chain Mount, Quartet Chain Mount,
                             Heavy Chain Mount, Heavy Slope Ceiling Mount,
                             Ladder Rest

     Post & Pier:            Post Fitter, Grand Post Fitter,
                             Pier Mount, Grand Pier Mount,
                             Copper Pier Mount,
                             Direct Burial Post Adapter,
                             Lamp Posts (6/8/10 foot),
                             Post Ladder Rest

   Important rule: standard Wall Mount Accessories (Top Scroll,
   Bottom Scroll, hooks, etc.) attach to the standard backplate.
   So they're available ONLY when no non-standard Wall Mount is
   selected. Pick a Gooseneck or Grand Harbor and the Wall Mount
   Accessories disappear, and the other way around.

5. COMPANION (e.g. -CY05 paired with -EE1)
   Some mount options require a partner because they don't stand
   alone on the fixture:

     Estate Extension     requires a Ceiling & Hanging mount.
                          Any ceiling option available for the
                          selected base SKU is valid.
                          Output: AS41E-EE1-CY05

     Grand Harbor 12"     requires a Post Fitter (PF) or
       and Grand Harbor 17"   Grand Post Fitter (GPF).
                              Output: AS41E-GH1-PF1

   When you select one of these, the rest of its mount category
   hides and a companion picker appears with the right options.

6. DECORATIVE OPTIONS (e.g. -LTS, -BBR)
   Add-ons that sit on top of the chosen mount:
     London Top, London Bottom, Fluted Top,
     Solid Copper Top Panels, Fleur De Lis Finial,
     Braided Brass Ring, Coach House Bars

   You can combine London Top with London Bottom on the same SKU.
   See the Top + Bottom rule below.

7. ELECTRIC OR GAS SPECIFIC (goes at the END of the SKU)
   Electric options:
     Adhara Dark Sky (ADS), Turtle Friendly (TLA),
     Frosted Shade (HSI1), Sanded Shade (HSI2),
     Cylinder Hurricane Shade (CSHI)
   Gas options:
     Propane Tip (PT or SPTS), Gas Pressure Regulator (GPR),
     e-lyte (ELI), Auto Shutoff Valve (ASV),
     Wind Guard (WGS or WGL)


================================================================
THE TOP + BOTTOM RULE
================================================================

In Wall Mount Accessories and Decorative Options, accessories are
classified as "top" or "bottom." One top can combine with one bottom
on a single SKU. Two tops, or two bottoms, fork into separate SKUs.

Tops in Wall Mount Accessories:
  Top Scroll, Top Scroll Wide, Farm House Hook, Moneo Top Hook
Bottoms in Wall Mount Accessories:
  Bottom Scroll, Bottom Scroll Wide, Reverse Bottom Scroll,
  Bottom Farm House Hook

Tops in Decorative Options:
  London Top, Fluted Top, Solid Copper Top Panels
Bottoms in Decorative Options:
  London Bottom

Examples:
  Top Scroll + Bottom Scroll    = AS41E-TS3-BS1     (one SKU)
  Top Scroll + Top Scroll Wide  = AS41E-TS3  AND  AS41E-TSW3   (two SKUs)
  Farm House Hook + BFH         = AS41E-FH3-BFH2   (FH counts as a top)
  London Top + London Bottom    = AS41E-LTS-LBS    (one SKU)


================================================================
WORKED EXAMPLES
================================================================

  AS41E
    Adams Street, 41 inches, Electric. Base unit, antique copper,
    standard backplate. Wall mounted. Complete product.

  AS41E-BLK
    Same as above but matte black finish.

  AS41G-BLK-TS3
    Adams Street 41" Gas, matte black, with a Top Scroll (size TS3).

  AS43G-BLK-TS11
    Same product line, larger size (43"), Gas. With Top Scroll —
    notice the code is TS11, not TS3, because accessory codes
    differ per base SKU.

  AS41E-EE1-CY05
    Adams Street 41" Electric with Estate Extension wall mount
    paired with Classic Yoke as the required ceiling companion.

  AS41E-GH1-PF1
    Adams Street 41" Electric with Grand Harbor 12" wall mount
    paired with Post Fitter as the required post-fitter companion.

  AS41G-BLK-TS3-LTS-ASV
    Adams Street 41" Gas, matte black, Top Scroll, London Top,
    Auto Shutoff Valve. Note the gas option (ASV) comes last.

  AH22E-BMSM
    Biltmore Antler Hill 22" Electric with Biltmore Stem Mount.
    Biltmore lines use BM-prefixed hardware (BMTS, BMCM, BMPF, BMPM,
    BMY, BMCHM, BMSM, BMCPM) instead of the standard codes.

  AOB28E
    Biltmore Aurora Brass 28" Electric. No finish suffix because
    AOB is brass-only.


================================================================
THINGS THAT CONFUSE PEOPLE
================================================================

* The accessory CODES are different across sizes within the same
  family. A Top Scroll on AS41 is TS3, on AS43 is TS11. There's no
  universal "Top Scroll = TS" — the code depends on the base SKU
  you're attaching it to.

* The PARENT SKU never appears in the buildable SKU. If you see
  AS-E-AS41E somewhere, that's wrong. It's just AS41E.

* Biltmore products use different mount hardware from the standard
  collections. You can't put a regular Wall Yoke on a Biltmore
  fixture. Biltmore lines have BMTS, BMCM, BMPF, BMPM, BMY, BMCHM,
  BMSM, BMCPM. The Biltmore Stem Mount code is BMSM (not BSSM).

* Wall Mount and Wall Mount Accessories are MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE.
  Pick a Gooseneck and you lose access to the Top Scroll. Pick a
  Top Scroll and you lose access to the Gooseneck. They attach to
  the same place on the fixture.

* Estate Extension is not a complete mount on its own. It always
  needs a ceiling/hanging companion appended to the SKU.

* Grand Harbor 12" and Grand Harbor 17" are not complete mounts
  on their own. They always need a Post Fitter or Grand Post
  Fitter appended to the SKU.

* The AOB family is the only brass-only Biltmore line. AO (without
  the B) is the copper Aurora, and it takes finishes like everything
  else.


================================================================
WHERE THE TRUTH LIVES
================================================================

The authoritative source for every accessory code on every base SKU
is CopperSmith's master product sheet (CS_Master_Product_List).
Every base SKU is a row, every accessory is a column. The cell at
their intersection is the exact code.

If this document and the live builder ever disagree with the master
sheet, the master sheet is right.
